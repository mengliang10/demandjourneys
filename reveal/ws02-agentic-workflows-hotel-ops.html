03 / High-Yield Playbooks

The 4 Core Hospitality Agentic Workflows

Specific implementations deployed in multi-property hotel environments

1. Autonomous Parity Sentinel Agent

Trigger: Runs every 30 minutes across Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, and 12 metasearch engines.

Action: Detects unauthorized OTA rate unbundling / wholesale leakage. Takes automated timestamped screenshots, logs the exact distributor code, and sends a pre-drafted breach notice to the market manager.

Eliminates 92% of rogue rate leakage

2. Instant Corporate MICE RFP Closer

Trigger: Inbound corporate event inquiry received via email or webform.

Action: Ingests meeting room capacity, calculates F&B minimum spend, checks block availability in PMS, and generates a personalized, branded PDF proposal within 3 minutes.

Lifts lead-to-booking conversion by +38%

3. VIP Guest Synthesis & Briefing Agent

Trigger: Daily 06:00 AM PMS check-in manifest generation.

Action: Aggregates previous stay history, dining preferences, linked corporate entity, social presence, and flags specific high-value repeat guests for personalized GM welcome notes and tailored amenities.

+42% NPS score among top 5% spenders

4. Intelligent Pre-Stay Monetization Agent

Trigger: T-minus 72 hours before arrival.

Action: Analyzes arrival flight time and current suite occupancy. Offers targeted early check-in, premium airport limousine transfer, and curated dinner reservations via WhatsApp.

+S$48 Incremental Net Margin per Room
Practical application: - Ask attendees: "How long does it currently take your sales team to reply to a 50-room corporate retreat RFP? If it takes 24-48 hours, you have already lost the booking to competitor hotels that respond in minutes."
04 / Roadmap & Governance

The 90-Day Agentic Deployment Sprint

Structured implementation timeline with strict risk and privacy guardrails

Month 1: Tool Hooks & Read-Only Agents

  • Connect API connectors to PMS (Opera/Cloudbeds), CRS, and email inbox.
  • Deploy read-only Parity Sentinel Agent in audit mode.
  • Establish strict data isolation (zero guest PII fed to public model training).

Month 2: Human-in-the-Loop Drafting

  • Deploy Corporate RFP Assistant with DOSM approval queue.
  • Implement guest review response generator for GM review.
  • Benchmark time savings and output quality.

Month 3: Autonomous Execution & Scale

  • Enable automated pre-stay WhatsApp upsell triggers.
  • Integrate automated RMS rule adjustments during peak demand surges.
  • Roll out across portfolio properties with centralized monitoring.
