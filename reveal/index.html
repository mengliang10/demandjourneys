02 / The Neutrality Principle

The Channel Is Not the Answer.

Demand Journeys does not promote dogmatic channel philosophies. Every channel has a specific commercial role.

Direct is not automatically better.
High paid-search bidding, discounts, tech licenses and loyalty overhead can easily exceed third-party commission costs.
OTA is not automatically worse.
OTAs provide global billboard reach, international currency settlement, and low-friction discovery in feeder markets you cannot efficiently target directly.
Paid media is not automatically inefficient.
When focused on incremental high-intent demand and high-margin shoulder periods, paid media delivers outstanding commercial yield.
CRM is not automatically valuable.
Mass email blasts with unsegmented promotions erode brand equity and train customers to wait for discounts.
The Golden Rule: Evaluate every channel strictly by the incremental net commercial value it produces relative to the total cost required to generate it.
DEMAND JOURNEYS The Channel Neutrality Principle
Purpose: Differentiate from biased agencies or anti-OTA zealots. Position as a balanced commercial advisor who respects both direct and indirect economics.

Talking Points:

  • Conversely, relying 100% on OTAs for repeat local corporate guests is leaving massive margin on the table.
