# Demand Journeys — Executive Growth & Marketing Workshop Deck

Interactive Reveal.js executive workshop presentation for **Demand Journeys**, a Singapore-based marketing and growth advisory practice founded by **Tan Meng Liang** (Fractional CMO / CDMO).

---

## 🎯 Executive Overview

The **Demand Journeys Executive Growth & Marketing Workshop** is a proprietary 90-minute executive working session designed for hotel owners, holding companies, hospitality executives, General Managers, Commercial Leaders, Marketing Directors, and Revenue Managers.

It helps organisations answer two fundamental commercial questions:
1. **"Where does demand actually come from, and what does it cost to generate?"**
2. **"How do you deliver the best experience for the customer's money, at an optimised cost?"**

## ⌨️ Presenter Navigation & Keybindings

- **`Right Arrow` / `Space`**: Next slide
- **`Left Arrow`**: Previous slide
- **`S`**: Open **Speaker Notes Window** (displays slide notes, timers, upcoming slide preview)
- **`O` / `ESC`**: Toggle **Slide Overview Mode** (grid view of all 28 slides)
- **`F`**: Toggle **Fullscreen Mode**
- **`?`**: Show Reveal.js Help Modal

---

## 🎙️ Speaker Notes Built-In

Every slide includes a rich `<aside class="notes">` block structured with:
- **Purpose**: Strategic objective of the slide.
- **Talking Points**: Concrete points for the executive presenter.
- **Executive Question**: Interactive probe to engage the room.
- **Transition**: Verbal bridge to the next slide.
