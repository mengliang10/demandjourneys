## 5-Page Slide Structure

### Page 01: Executive Profile & Value Proposition
- **Title:** Tan Meng Liang — Performance Marketing · Platform Transformation · Commercial Accountability.
- **Remit:** For DBS · SVP/VP, AdTech Lead, Performance Marketing Platforms.
- **Track Record:** 20+ years digital commerce and acquisition leadership; S$5.5M Board-approved Project Meta programme at Frasers; US$15M+ annual APAC media remit at HomeAway / Expedia Group.
- **Career Chronology:** Frasers Hospitality (2018–2024), HomeAway (2016–2018), Accor (2013–2016), TripAdvisor (2010–2012).

### Page 02: Experience Relevant to DBS
- **Platform Transformation (Frasers):** Business case and delivery of S$5.5M Project Meta combining website, CRS, and CRM investment with direct digital media. Application: translating marketing needs into investment cases and joint acceptance decisions.
- **Regional Governance (Accor):** Named account and budget responsibility for regional SEM; coordinated agency access and Kenshoo handoffs. Application: DBS-owned accounts, clear agency access, market decision rights, and repeatable AdOps standards.
- **Performance Leadership (HomeAway):** Directed US$15M+ media investment across 7 APAC markets with an 8-person team. Application: connecting signal quality, experimentation, and budget choices to qualified customer outcomes.
- **Customer Data / CDP Scope:** Five-year customer-data roadmap, RFP, and data integration across ~2.3M profiles and 12 legacy systems.

### Page 03: Proposed Operating Approach
- **Platform Lifecycle:**
  1. *Define:* Business outcome, qualified conversions, value, exclusions, and measurement windows.
  2. *Govern:* DBS account ownership, least-privilege permissions, permitted data fields, purpose/consent controls.
  3. *Deliver:* Platform-specific API mappings, audience synchronization, conversion feedback, creative templates, budget controls.
  4. *Learn:* Event reconciliation to business records, incrementality vs attribution, signal health monitoring.
- **Delivery Ownership:** Product brief, event contract, acceptance criteria, non-functional requirements (deduplication, latency, retries, error alerts, rollback).

### Page 04: Proposed First 90 Days Delivery Plan
- **Days 1–30 (Diagnose & Agree):** Inventory accounts, access, contracts, tags, feeds, and conversion definitions; map agency vs internal responsibilities; select pilot candidate. *Gate: Sponsor, data use, capacity, and pilot scope agreed.*
- **Days 31–60 (Specify & Prototype):** Write event schema, API mappings, test cases, and non-functional requirements; test with synthetic/approved data; define creative metadata and incident procedures. *Gate: Joint Technology, Data, and control-owner review.*
- **Days 61–90 (Pilot & Decide):** Run capped pilot; reconcile events, latency, spend controls, and business outcomes; agree capability transfer and next investment tranche. *Gate: Expand, fix, or stop based on evidence.*

### Page 05: Beyond Day 90 — Scaling & Unified Scorecard
- **Months 4–6 (Make Pilot Repeatable):** Expand proven connectors; bring repeatable QA, reporting, and trafficking in-house; introduce conversion feedback and incrementality tests.
- **Months 7–12 (Add Intelligence with Accountability):** Evaluate predictive value signals with Data Science (labels, drift, calibration); deploy AI assistance for briefs, creative variants, and diagnostics with human review; enforce spend ceilings and kill switches.
- **Unified Scorecard:**
  - *Control:* Access exceptions, launch defect rate, incident resolution.
  - *Signal:* Source-to-received completeness, freshness, event reconciliation.
  - *Delivery:* Release lead time, internal service levels, total operating cost.
  - *Value:* Cost per qualified outcome, incremental gross contribution.
