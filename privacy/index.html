Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Demand Journeys handles information submitted through this website.

Information Collected

When you submit an enquiry through the contact form, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, the selected topic or issue category, and your message description.

Please do not include confidential, commercially sensitive, or personal data beyond what is required to initiate contact.

How Your Information Is Used

Information submitted through this website is used solely to evaluate, respond to, and communicate with you regarding your commercial or advisory enquiry. We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information.

Third-Party Services

The enquiry form on this website is processed securely via FormSubmit. Information transmitted is delivered to Demand Journeys via email at ml@demandjourneys.com.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or wish to request an update or removal of information you previously submitted, please contact Meng Liang Tan directly at ml@demandjourneys.com.
