## Directional recommendation
Advance **B (Journey Path)** as the lead design direction. Keep **A (Refined D/J)** as the reference for brand continuity, and **C** archived as an explored alternative. Refine B for a compact, distinctive silhouette, balanced trajectory, few nodes and reliable small-size reproduction. Avoid decorative leaves, unnecessary dots, fine strokes and overly ornate ligatures in small-scale versions. Check whether any journey motif can be embedded within the D/J letters without breaking letter recognition. Keep type separate as an editable system during exploration and convert to outlines only for approved final export.

## Required approval tests
1. One-colour black and inverse white at 16px, 24px, 32px and 48px.
2. Wordmark and mark-only horizontal/stacked use at native sizes.
3. 16:9 presentation header/footer, website hero/navigation, A4 document header, social avatar.
4. Newsreader/DM Sans brand font consistency; licensed font handling.
5. Distinctiveness review and trademark clearance before commercial launch.
6. Do not revise live favicon or production header until owner selects the master.
