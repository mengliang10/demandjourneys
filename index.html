The Business Problem

Marketing Investment Is Too Important to Manage on Intuition Alone.

Hospitality businesses spend significant capital on marketing, but often lack an independent view of what that investment is actually contributing to demand.

Ownership and executive leadership need to evaluate marketing with the same commercial rigour applied to capital allocation. Yet, critical questions often remain unanswered:

01

Incremental vs Baseline Demand

Which marketing activities are creating genuine incremental bookings, and which are simply taking credit for demand that would have booked anyway?

02

Channel Over-Crediting

Are last-click attribution models and agency dashboards over-crediting lower-funnel channels at the expense of true demand creators?

03

Diminishing Returns

Where has media investment reached saturation, and where is the marginal cost of customer acquisition rising above sustainable yields?

04

Uncaptured Opportunity

Where do under-invested markets, direct distribution channels, or emerging discovery engines represent high-return opportunities?

05

Budget Reallocation

How should marketing capital be reallocated across properties, regions, and channels to maximise aggregate portfolio returns?

06

Defensible Investment Cases

How can marketing leadership present a clear, evidence-based business case to ownership and holding boards for future budget approvals?
