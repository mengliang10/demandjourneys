Marketing Measurement & Profitability

Big Picture Modeling & Customer Journey Tracking

Stop guessing which marketing channels actually drive profit. Get clear, audit-ready numbers on where your next ad dollar should go.

Big Picture Marketing Model (MMM)

The Executive View: Shows how your overall marketing, seasonal trends, room rates, and competitor ads impact your bottom line.

  • Ad Memory Effect: Tracks how ads placed today continue generating sales over the next few weeks.
  • Spend Limits: Identifies when adding more budget to a channel stops giving you extra profit.
  • Smart Budget Reallocation: Reallocates your budget to channels that bring in the highest return.

Customer Journey Revenue Audit (MTA)

The Sales Audit View: Follows actual customer steps from first click to final booking, dividing revenue credit fairly across channels.

  • Channel Impact Test: Measures how many sales you would lose if you shut off a specific ad channel.
  • Fair Sales Credit: Gives proper credit to touchpoints that assisted sales instead of just the last click.
  • Automated Reports: Delivers simple, daily sales dashboards your finance team can trust.
