Primary Vertical Specialization

Make Hotel Demand More Profitable.

Demand Journeys helps hotel owners, operators and hospitality businesses understand the economics connecting demand generation, direct booking, distribution, customer data, technology and commercial performance.

Hotels rarely have a single demand problem. They have interconnected challenges across OTA dependency, direct booking, acquisition cost, conversion, loyalty, customer data, technology, distribution and measurement.

Optimizing one function in isolation simply moves the cost somewhere else. Demand Journeys evaluates the system as a whole.

01

Direct vs. OTA Economics

Understand the true economics of direct and indirect channels, including acquisition costs, commission structures, discount promotions and downstream customer value.

02

Distribution Strategy

Evaluate whether the current channel mix produces the right balance of reach, room revenue, net ADR and operating margin across properties.

03

Digital Commerce & Booking Flow

Identify friction in the digital booking journey, CRS/booking engine drop-offs, and opportunities to increase direct conversion and basket contribution.

04

Customer & Loyalty

Connect CRM, loyalty programs and first-party guest data to direct acquisition, repeat stays and compounding lifetime value.

05

MarTech & Customer Platforms

Determine whether the existing tech stack (CDP, CRM, PMS, CRS, analytics) actually enables the commercial strategy or drains operating budget.

06

Measurement & Incrementality

Move beyond channel-reported performance toward a clearer understanding of true incremental commercial contribution across paid search, metasearch and display.

07

AI & Emerging Discovery

Assess how AI-mediated discovery, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), AI Overviews (AEO/AIO) and emerging agentic booking journeys impact future hotel demand and distribution.

Practical Scenarios

What Clients Engage Demand Journeys to Solve

Situations where commercial, marketing, technology and data disconnects require senior diagnostic clarity and operator leadership.

Unclear Incremental Demand

You do not know which customers, channels, journeys and technology investments are actually producing profitable incremental demand versus cannibalizing existing revenue.

Marketing, revenue management, distribution, customer data and technology teams operate in departmental silos with misaligned incentives and metrics.

Value Leakage in Customer Journey

Customer acquisition is happening, but digital conversion drop-offs, poor booking flow, or weak CRM retention are leaking margin throughout the lifecycle.
