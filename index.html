When I can help

You know something needs to change.
You may not know what.

You do not need to arrive with a diagnosis. Start with what you are seeing in the business, or the opportunity you want to pursue.

01 / Does this sound familiar?

We need growth. Our current approach is not delivering.

You may be entering a market, struggling to generate qualified enquiries or losing customers to competitors.

How I help I examine your customers, offer, positioning and routes to market, then identify where marketing and sales need to change.

So you can decide Which customers to prioritise, how to reach them and what will help them choose you.

02 / Does this sound familiar?

There is plenty of activity. We cannot tell what works.

Teams and agencies report results, but the reports do not explain what customers cost or why sales changed.

How I help I connect marketing and customer data with commercial costs, investigate where business is being lost and test the assumptions behind reported performance.

So you can decide What to continue, what to investigate and where to redirect effort.

03 / Does this sound familiar?

The technology is there. The business benefit is not.

A new platform, a stalled project or disconnected teams are absorbing time without making the business work better.

How I help I review what the business needs, what your systems can do and how the work gets done. Then I help you choose and organise the changes.

So you can decide What to fix, what to replace and who needs to deliver it.

Develop B2B or B2C demand generation across content, partnerships, campaigns and search. Improve visibility in search engines (SEO), answer engines (AEO) and generative AI search (GEO), then connect that visibility to enquiries and sales.

DEMAND GENERATION · SEO · AEO · GEO

03 Turn more interest into sales and repeat business.

Find where people drop out of the buying journey and where follow-up falls short. Use customer economics to guide conversion improvements, CRM, loyalty and retention activity.

CUSTOMER ECONOMICS · CONVERSION · RETENTION

04 Get a clearer answer about what is working.

Reconcile conflicting reports, examine acquisition costs and customer value, and use experiments where needed. Establish what the evidence supports before increasing, moving or reducing investment.

ANALYSIS · MEASUREMENT · EXPERIMENTATION

05 Choose technology around the business need.

Define requirements, review what you already have and compare vendor proposals. For MarTech, travel and hospitality platforms, assess integration, adoption and practical AI uses alongside the commercial case.

MARTECH · TRAVELTECH · HOSPITALITY PLATFORMS · AI

06 Get teams and partners delivering the same priorities.

Set clear ownership, measures and working routines across marketing, sales, commercial and technology teams. Provide advisory or fractional leadership to coordinate people, agencies and vendors through delivery.

OPERATING MODELS · LEADERSHIP · PARTNER MANAGEMENT
