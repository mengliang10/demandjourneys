RESEARCH DOSSIER · SG1

Visual discovery and qualified-enquiry generation for Singapore luxury-travel audiences.

SG · Discovery · proposed · updated 2026-11-22

Channel objective

The original blueprint proposes Reels and paid social to communicate access and itineraries that are difficult to discover through conventional searches. Creative should highlight specialists and the nature of the service, with enquiries routed to a consistent CRM process.

Execution sequence

  1. Define prospect segments and their travel motivations.
  2. Build original visual assets with documented usage rights.
  3. Test creative concepts against qualified-enquiry outcomes.
  4. Capture campaign source and maintain the consent record.
  5. Analyze booking yield rather than clicks alone.

Metrics

Track CTR, saves and CPL alongside qualified enquiry, booking conversion and revenue. The 30% market-channel allocation shown in the original HTML is a proposal, not observed spend.
