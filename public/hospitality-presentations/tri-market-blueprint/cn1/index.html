Strategic role

The source blueprint positions Xiaohongshu as a discovery channel for travel planning in China. Its proposed program uses destination-specific notes, creator content and specialist voices to build interest before moving qualified conversations to WeChat or the CRM.

Content execution

  • Publish native 3:4 notes with useful destination detail rather than generic luxury imagery.
  • Combine brand/specialist accounts with vetted KOCs and KOLs.
  • Organize content around destination-specific queries such as Tibet, Xinjiang, aurora and Bhutan.
  • Capture campaign identifiers before handing off platform enquiries.

Measurement

The blueprint identifies saves, search rank and direct messages as channel-level signals. Qualified enquiries, booked revenue and acquisition cost should be measured downstream. Targets require baseline validation.
