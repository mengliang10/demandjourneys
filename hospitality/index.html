Recognise the situation?

I will help you work out what to change.

01 / Demand & distribution

Bookings are growing. Acquisition costs are growing with them.

You need to know whether the mix of guests, channels and offers is improving what each stay contributes.

How I help I compare guest segments and booking channels using consistent costs, then review investment, incentives and distribution choices with your team.

So you can decide Which channels and guests to prioritise, and what to test before increasing spend.

02 / Brand, discovery & booking

Your website attracts interest. Too few guests book.

The constraint could be visibility, the offer or the booking experience. Another campaign may not address it.

How I help I trace the journey from search and AI discovery through your website to the booking engine. I review positioning, content and conversion, then prioritise changes with your team and partners.

So you can decide Whether to generate more demand, strengthen the offer or fix the path to booking.

03 / Platforms & guest relationships

A platform promises better results. The business case is unclear.

A website, booking engine, CRM or customer-data proposal needs a clearer purpose than “modernisation”.

How I help I define the guest and business needs, assess existing capabilities and compare proposals. I assess how each option could support loyalty, repeat stays and personalisation, and clarify integration needs and team responsibilities.

So you can decide What to buy, what to improve within the existing stack and what must be in place for it to work.

04 / Commercial leadership

Everyone is busy. The important changes still stall.

Marketing, revenue, sales and technology have separate plans. Agencies report results, but decisions and delivery move slowly.

How I help I help establish shared measures, clear ownership and regular reviews. Advisory or fractional leadership can coordinate your team and partners around the agreed priorities.

So you can decide Who owns the next change, what support they need and how you will judge progress.

A practical engagement

You do not need
all the answers to start.

An introductory conversation can establish whether a diagnostic, targeted advisory or delivery support fits the situation.

01 Who is this for?

Independent hotels, owners, management companies, regional groups and established brands. We can focus on one property, one channel or one decision, as well as wider portfolio needs.

02 Do we need clean data before we start?

No. Existing channel reports, booking journeys, guest segments or a technology overview can help. If the evidence is incomplete, we start with discovery and identify what needs to be measured.

03 Does the diagnostic include implementation?

The initial session produces a working demand map, leakage points, measurement gaps and a maturity scorecard. Deeper analysis, detailed roadmaps and implementation support are separate engagements.

04 Can you work with our existing team and partners?

Yes. The work can involve your GM, commercial, revenue, marketing, sales and technology teams, alongside existing agencies and platform vendors. Responsibilities are agreed around the problem.
