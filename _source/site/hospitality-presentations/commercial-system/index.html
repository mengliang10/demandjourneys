01 / WHY A SYSTEM

The problem is rarely the channel

The same customer moves through every stage. A gain in one can create a cost in another.

THE CHANNEL VIEW

Each team optimises its own metric

Media buys reach. The site optimises conversion. Distribution protects margin. Technology owns the stack. Each decision looks reasonable on its own report.

THE SYSTEM VIEW

The customer sees one business

Demand, customers, channels, distribution, technology, data and measurement interact as one commercial system.

The method starts with where value leaks, not with which channel to buy next.
