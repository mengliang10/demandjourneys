The commercial question

The right channel is the one that brings a valuable guest
at an economically sensible cost.

01 / Channel economics

Are direct bookings actually more profitable?

Compare direct, OTA, metasearch and other distribution routes after acquisition costs, commissions, discounts, payment fees and servicing costs. Find the mix that makes commercial sense for each property and guest segment.

02 / Demand & brand

Which guests should you build demand for?

Connect positioning and brand to the guests, corporate accounts and occasions worth pursuing. Align B2B and B2C demand generation, paid media, partnerships and content with the value of the stay.

03 / Discovery & conversion

Can the right guest find you — and book?

Review SEO, answer engine optimisation (AEO), generative engine optimisation (GEO), metasearch and Brand.com. Identify where search visibility, website content or the booking engine loses valuable demand.

04 / Guest value

Does the relationship last beyond checkout?

Connect segmentation, CRM, loyalty and customer data to repeat stays and lifetime contribution. Assess whether incentives build a valuable guest relationship and whether your teams can act on the data.

05 / Platforms & investment

Is the hotel technology stack earning its keep?

Evaluate property management, central reservations, booking engines, CRM, CDP and other hospitality platforms. Test vendor proposals against guest needs, integration effort, operating costs and practical use cases for AI.

06 / People & measurement

Are the commercial teams working from the same picture?

Align owners, GMs, revenue, marketing, sales and technology teams around common measures and clear decisions. Improve reporting, partner governance and operating workflows so insight changes what happens next.

01 Who is this for?

Independent hotels, owners, management companies, regional groups and established brands. The scope can centre on one property, a portfolio or a shared commercial capability.

02 Do we need clean data before we start?

No. Existing channel reports, booking journeys, guest segments or a technology overview can help. If the evidence is incomplete, we start with discovery and identify what needs to be measured.

03 Does the diagnostic include implementation?

The initial session produces a working demand map, leakage points, measurement gaps and a maturity scorecard. Deeper analysis, detailed roadmaps and implementation support are separate engagements.

04 Can you work with our existing team and partners?

Yes. The work can involve your GM, commercial, revenue, marketing, sales and technology teams, alongside existing agencies and platform vendors. Responsibilities are agreed around the problem.
