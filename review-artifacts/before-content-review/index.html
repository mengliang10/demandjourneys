Where profit gets lost

A growing business can still
be leaking value.

More leads, more bookings and more technology can all look like progress. The commercial question is what remains after the cost of winning, serving and keeping the customer.

01 /

Demand

The wrong audience or an undifferentiated offer.

02 /

Acquisition

Activity that costs more than the value it creates.

03 /

Conversion

Interested customers who never become buyers.

04 /

Distribution

Revenue diluted by fees, discounts and channel costs.

05 /

Retention

One-time transactions with little lasting value.

06 /

Measurement

Good-looking reports that lead to poor decisions.

Bring us your question ↗
01 Where should we compete — and why should customers choose us?

Clarify the customers, markets and unmet needs worth pursuing. Connect brand positioning, the offer and marketing strategy to the value customers are willing to pay for.

MARKETING STRATEGY · BRANDING · CUSTOMER ANALYSIS

02 Where will the next valuable customer come from?

Build demand across B2B and B2C: market education, content, partnerships, organic discovery and paid channels. Assess search engine optimisation (SEO), answer engine optimisation (AEO) and generative engine optimisation (GEO) within the wider customer journey.

DEMAND GENERATION · SEO · AEO · GEO

03 Which customers are worth acquiring — and keeping?

Look beyond the first transaction. Understand contribution margin, acquisition cost, repeat behaviour and lifetime value, then improve conversion, CRM, loyalty and retention around the customers that matter.

CUSTOMER ECONOMICS · LIFECYCLE · CONVERSION

04 What actually caused the growth?

Separate reported performance from incremental impact. Use commercial analysis, attribution, experimentation and customer cohorts to make better decisions about what to fund, improve or stop.

ANALYSIS · MEASUREMENT · INCREMENTALITY

05 Which technology earns its place in the business?

Evaluate MarTech, travel technology and hospitality platforms against real use cases, integration needs, adoption and cost. Assess where customer data and AI can improve economics before committing to another platform.

MARTECH · TRAVELTECH · HOSPITALITY TECH · AI

06 Can the organisation deliver the strategy?

Connect marketing, sales, commercial teams and technology through clear ownership, decision rights, workflows and measures. Design an operating model that people can actually use.

OPERATING MODELS · TEAM CAPABILITY · PARTNER GOVERNANCE
