# Fractional GEO, AEO & SEO — Demand Journeys

28-slide Reveal.js presentation with two anonymised financial-services diagnostic cases. Uses the warm ivory, deep green and teal palette of the Personal Portfolio.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Arrow keys navigate; `O` opens overview and `F` fullscreen. The contents page links to chapters. The header includes a PDF download. All rendering assets are local, so the deck works offline. External reference and email links require connectivity.

## Interaction
- Slide 15: change the initial business constraint to see a corresponding first-sprint recommendation.
- Slide 21: adjust cost, contribution and conversion assumptions to calculate the additional qualified leads needed for break-even. Inputs are illustrative, not prices or results.
- The PDF captures the default examples and explains that controls are static.

## Editorial scope
The two case studies describe findings reported in supplied April 2026 research. This is not a new audit of the organisations, a claim of implemented work or evidence of realised AI-citation/revenue uplift. Company names, domains, executive names, product brands, unique ranking positions and precise company financials are excluded.

The deck uses SEO, AEO and GEO as operational definitions. The archive used AIO inconsistently, including for answer engines and agent readiness. Agent integrations are treated as optional and separate from the core scope.

## Evidence decisions
- Excluded simulated citation shares, unsupported market volumes, inferred performance scores and promised uplift.
- A sitemap count is not an indexed-page count; an endpoint returning 404 does not prove that every sitemap is missing.
- A partial HTML fetch does not establish how every crawler renders the site.
- Structured data is not a prerequisite for Google AI inclusion. It should match visible content and applicable requirements.
- No promised FAQ rich-result gain, llms.txt ranking gain, Wikipedia placement or paid editorial result.
- No blanket domain merger, training-crawler permission or platform rebuild prescribed.
- Kept brand mention, owned-source citation, checkable-claim accuracy and commercial measures separate.
- Historical pricing variants were not carried forward as current commercial offers.
